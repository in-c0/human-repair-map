## D1 — The Annotated Atlas
Thesis: a contemporary anatomical atlas whose annotations are capabilities, evidence,
dependencies, questions. Archival, exact, warm, physical; paper not white UI.
Light: paper #F3F0E7 · sheet #FBF9F3 · ink #1B1A18 · ink-2 #5E5A52 · rule #CFC8BA · teal #315F61 · unreviewed ochre #9A681E · disputed oxide #963F32 · reviewed green #426451.
Dark: ground #141513 · sheet #1A1B18 · ink #E8E2D5 · ink-2 #AAA498 · rule #3E403A · teal #78A8A5 · ochre #D2A35D · oxide #D17D6D · green #82AD8D. "An atlas plate on a dim light table, not a black control room."
Type: Source Serif 4 (titles, statements) + IBM Plex Sans Condensed (labels, leader captions, column heads) + IBM Plex Mono (ids, hashes, routes only).
Layout: entrance = opening spread of an atlas, plate index left, two large horizontal cuts (Universal Repair / Rejuvenation) over a faint shared structure; plate inscription along a rule carries version/records/reviewed/snapshot and the non-clinical line. Map = annotated plate: goals as anchors, capabilities on leader lines, ranked questions as numbered marginal annotations, L0–L5 as one ruler. Record = specimen plate: prose column with marginal annotations, provenance as a vertical evidence margin; no sticky infobox. Grid = an atlas plate with marks/hatch/position, not filled rectangles.
Motion: mechanical registration, cubic-bezier(.20,.76,.24,1), 320–480 ms (650 structural). Signature: switching maps — shared capabilities stay registered in place while projection-specific annotations withdraw and enter; for ~0.5 s you see two maps cut from one graph.
Lazy AI would: sepia textures, engravings, handwriting, veins, ornaments. Refused: the atlas is label discipline, leader lines, plate numbering, margins, registration.

## D2 — The Frontier Ledger
Thesis: the instrument researchers keep score with; every capability has a position, every claim provenance, every change a trace. Tufte / OWID / versioned ledger.
Light: field #F7F6F0 · ink #17191A · secondary #555B59 · rule #D2D1C8 · ledger green #28594B · research blue #41687B · ochre #98691F · rust #994735.
Dark: field #111513 · ink #E6E8E2 · secondary #9EA59F · rule #353B37 · green #79AA91 · blue #83AABD · ochre #D0A459 · rust #D37E6D. Colour subordinate to position, rule weight, type.
Type: Spectral + Fira Sans + IBM Plex Mono.
Layout: entrance = two enormous ledger entries (01 UNIVERSAL REPAIR: scope, coverage, binding-constraint count, one representative question; 02 REJUVENATION), one ruled ledger row below. Map = each goal a horizontal section, capabilities as indented rows, AND/OR as brackets, every capability a mark on ONE common L0–L5 axis (`Dermal architecture ───────●`), hollow = unreviewed, solid only after human review; ranked question beneath the capability it constrains, hairline-connected. Record = large evidence ruler first, then observation → claim → source → replication → implication, with a small commit ledger. Grid = analytical matrix: minimal borders, strong row labels, tiny marks, stable columns, keyboard movement.
Motion: cubic-bezier(.16,1,.30,1), 180–320 ms (400 max). Signature: opening provenance traces a thin line from grade → claim → source → snapshot; nothing else animates.
Lazy AI would: KPI dashboard (tiles, progress bars, mini charts, trending arrows). Refused: a number appears only when the reader can inspect the evidence beneath it.

## D3 — The Dependency Survey  ← ChatGPT's recommendation
Thesis: draw the frontier as a surveyed dependency structure; the important objects are the connections we know, the gates they pass through, and the places where knowledge stops. Ancestry: atlas + engineering drawing + git history + dependency diagram. **Fifth element: the unknown as a first-class graphic object — negative knowledge, the frontier drawn by the shape of what is missing.**
Light: survey paper #F4F2EA · ink #18201F · secondary #59615D · construction line #CACCC4 · deep blue #36596B · muted moss #526B58 · unreviewed amber #A06B20 · disputed red #9C4339.
Dark: ground #111716 · ink #E6E5DC · secondary #9FA7A1 · construction #39413D · blue #7FA6B8 · moss #8AA18D · amber #D1A25D · red #D27C70.
Type: STIX Two Text (scientific authority; AND/OR logic feels native) + Work Sans (nav, labels) + Fragment Mono (addresses, hashes, routes only).
Layout: entrance = a quiet survey drawing fills the screen; one origin HUMAN REPAIR, two labelled traversals (Universal Repair, Rejuvenation) emerging from it over just enough of the shared graph; selecting changes which dependencies are emphasised; along one edge `0 / 326 HUMAN-REVIEWED` with the word UNREVIEWED spelled out. Map = NO node graph, no circles, no physics: orthogonal/elbowed lines, text is the node, AND/OR as explicit junctions, a blocking question drawn as a discontinuity in the line (`capability A ─── ? ─── capability B`). Record = opens with its coordinate (`Scarless skin repair / Dermis / Native ECM organisation`), slim dependency trace at left, prose centre, provenance as a vertical trace at right (grade │ claim │ experiment │ source │ snapshot hash). Grid = survey of coverage; each cell a glyph with rung position + separate review treatment; selecting opens the dependency trace, not a modal.
Motion: follows dependency, never decoration. cubic-bezier(.22,.68,.26,1), 220–360 ms (480 branch). Signature: opening a blocking question — the gap widens slightly, everything downstream quiets, the question expands in place (unknown / what it blocks / what would close it); closing restores. "Inspecting a broken circuit on a scientific drawing."
Lazy AI would: Three.js/SVG force-directed graph with glowing circles. Refused: deterministic reading order, fixed geometry, typography as data, explicit gates, drawn gaps; works when printed.

## Recommendation (ChatGPT)
D3 as the base, borrowing D1's label discipline and D2's common L0–L5 ruler. Reasons: expresses the graph instead of decorating it; 326 records live in deterministic structures not cards; AND/OR obvious; one stable measurement; review a second unmistakable dimension; ranked questions have a visible reason; the grid fits; git/hash provenance native; the machine face looks like the same product.
