Human Repair Roadmap

Map v0.1

A public, AI-assisted map of humanity’s progress toward preventing, treating, repairing, and eventually reversing major forms of biological damage.

Public-interest knowledge commons Snapshot: 11 June 2026 All changes auditable

Human repair is not one cure. It is a coordinated set of capabilities for reading biological state, defining a safe target, reaching the right cells, changing their state, and verifying that repair worked. This map makes those dependencies visible—and gives researchers, funders, patients, engineers, and citizens a shared place to improve the evidence.

i Scope: scientific capability mapping, evidence summaries, uncertainty, bottlenecks, and research coordination. This project does not diagnose, recommend treatments, or forecast a “universal cure date.”
